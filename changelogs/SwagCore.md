## 🛠️ FleaMC Update - New Features & Quality of Life!

We’ve got some awesome new tools and updates live on FleaMC! Here is what’s new:

* 🧍 **Armor Stand Editor:** Take full control of your builds! Use `/armorstand` (or `/as`) while looking at an armor stand to easily toggle gravity, size, arms, base plates, visibility, glowing effects, and custom names!
* 🎒 **Instant Condensing:** Got clogged inventory? Use the brand new `/condense` command to instantly turn your raw ingots, gems, and resources into compact blocks!
* ⏱️ **Playtime Tracking Upgrades:** We've overhauled how your playtime is tracked to make sure every single second of your adventure is accurately counted.
* 📜 **Rules Menu Refresh:** We've polished up the `/rules` menu and fixed some minor bugs behind the scenes to make reading server guidelines smoother than ever.
