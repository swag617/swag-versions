### 🛠️ SwagStack Update! 

We’ve rolled out an exciting backend update to our stacking and lag-clear systems to make your gameplay smoother and protect your hard-earned mobs! Here is what’s new:

* **🛡️ Smart Mob Clearing:** Our lag-clear system is now much smarter! It will now focus *only* on clearing mobs spawned from spawner blocks (the main source of grinder lag).
* **🐾 Pet & Wildlife Protection:** Naturally spawned wildlife, tamed pets, villagers, and wandering traders are now fully safe from being swept away during lag clears!
* **🧑‍🌾 Minion Safety:** Custom entities, like your farming minions, are now completely exempt from being cleared. 
* **📢 Broadcast Toggles:** We have streamlined how lag-clear warnings and notifications are broadcasted to the chat.
* **⚙️ Behind-the-Scenes Optimization:** We've upgraded our configuration and web systems to help staff manage stacking settings instantly with zero downtime or lag spikes.

Hop on now to experience the lag-free upgrades! **See you online!** 🎮👇
